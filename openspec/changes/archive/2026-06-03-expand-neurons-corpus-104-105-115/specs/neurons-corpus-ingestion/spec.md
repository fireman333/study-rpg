## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Corpus year coverage SHALL include 104–115 sittings

The reconciled corpus committed at `packages/content-neurons-tw/data/medexam-reconciled/questions.json` SHALL include, in addition to the existing 106-1 … 114-2 sittings, the following 一階 papers: **104-1, 104-2, 105-1, 105-2, 115-1**, each comprising the 醫學一 (`medexam-1`) and 醫學二 (`medexam-2`) papers. Every question SHALL carry `meta.year`, `meta.session`, `meta.book`, `meta.paper`, and `meta.qNumber`.

#### Scenario: New sittings present in reconciled corpus

- **WHEN** `data/medexam-reconciled/questions.json` is loaded
- **THEN** the set of distinct `(meta.year, meta.session)` pairs SHALL include `(104,1)`, `(104,2)`, `(105,1)`, `(105,2)`, and `(115,1)`
- **AND** each of those sittings SHALL contain questions for both `meta.paper === 'medexam-1'` and `meta.paper === 'medexam-2'`

#### Scenario: Mock-exam metadata surfaces the new papers

- **WHEN** the content build computes distinct `(year, session, paper)` triples
- **THEN** the 10 new triples (5 sittings × 2 papers) SHALL appear alongside the existing ones

### Requirement: 考選部 SHALL be the authoritative source for stem, options, and answer

For every ingested question, the `stem`, `options`, and `answer` fields SHALL be taken from the 考選部 official 試題 + 標準答案 (+ 更正答案) PDFs. 陽明 詳解 SHALL contribute ONLY the `explanation` field. When 考選部 and 陽明 disagree on stem/options/answer, the 考選部 value SHALL win.

#### Scenario: Answer derives from 考選部 standard/correction answer

- **WHEN** a question is reconciled for a year that has both 考選部 答案 and 陽明 詳解
- **THEN** `answer` SHALL equal the 考選部 標準答案 (as amended by 更正答案), independent of any answer stated in the 陽明 詳解

### Requirement: Explanation provenance SHALL follow one of two documented paths

Each question's explanation SHALL originate from exactly one of two paths, recorded so it is machine-distinguishable:

- **陽明-reconcile path** (sittings with published 陽明 詳解, e.g. 104/105/106–114): `explanation` is the cleaned 陽明 詳解; `sourceCredit` credits 考選部 (試題/答案) + 陽明國考考古題小組 (詳解, CC-BY-NC).
- **AI-generated path** (sittings without 陽明 詳解, e.g. 115-1): `explanation` is generated by an LLM; the question SHALL carry `explanationSource: 'ai-generated'`, and `sourceCredit` SHALL state the explanation is AI-generated and NOT 陽明-reviewed.

A question on the 陽明-reconcile path SHALL NOT carry `explanationSource: 'ai-generated'`.

#### Scenario: 115 questions are tagged AI-generated

- **WHEN** a 115-1 question with a non-empty explanation is loaded
- **THEN** it SHALL have `explanationSource === 'ai-generated'`
- **AND** its `sourceCredit` SHALL indicate the explanation is AI-generated and not 陽明-reviewed

#### Scenario: 陽明-sourced questions are not mislabelled

- **WHEN** a 104/105/106–114 question with a non-empty explanation is loaded
- **THEN** it SHALL NOT have `explanationSource === 'ai-generated'`

### Requirement: AI-generated explanations SHALL be answer-authoritative, verified, and low-confidence-flagged

The 115 explanation generator SHALL be given the 考選部 authoritative answer and SHALL produce an explanation that explains why that answer is correct — it SHALL NOT be permitted to choose or override the answer. After generation, a second-pass verification SHALL run, and any item whose generated reasoning contradicts the authoritative answer, or which the verification marks uncertain, SHALL be emitted to a low-confidence flag list for human review before the corpus is shipped.

#### Scenario: Generated explanation endorses the authoritative answer

- **WHEN** an explanation is generated for a 115 question whose 考選部 answer is `X`
- **THEN** the generator SHALL receive `X` as the correct answer to explain
- **AND** if the verification pass detects the explanation endorsing an answer other than `X`, that question SHALL be added to the low-confidence flag list

#### Scenario: Low-confidence list gated on human review

- **WHEN** the 115 generation + verification pass completes
- **THEN** a flag list of low-confidence / contradictory items SHALL be produced
- **AND** the corpus SHALL NOT be shipped until those flagged items have been reviewed

### Requirement: 舊分組 sittings SHALL use hand-confirmed contiguous subject blocks

For 一階 sittings predating the modern subject template (104-1, 104-2, 105-1, 105-2 — joining the existing 106-1), per-question subject SHALL be assigned from hand-confirmed contiguous `(startQ, endQ, subject)` blocks per (year, session, book), NOT from automatic classification. Every question in such a sitting SHALL resolve to exactly one subject; no question SHALL be left without a subject.

#### Scenario: Every old-grouping question resolves to exactly one subject

- **WHEN** the finalize step assigns subjects for 104-1 / 104-2 / 105-1 / 105-2
- **THEN** each `qNumber` SHALL fall within exactly one confirmed block for its book
- **AND** the resulting `subject` SHALL be one of the 10 canonical 一階 subjects
- **AND** no question SHALL have a null/empty subject

### Requirement: 更正答案 SHALL map to `disputed` or `acceptedAnswers`

Official 更正答案 SHALL be applied to the new sittings using the existing convention: 「一律給分」(any selection correct) SHALL set `disputed: true`; 「多選給分」(a specific set of letters accepted) SHALL set `acceptedAnswers: [<letters>]`.

#### Scenario: 115 醫學一 corrections applied

- **WHEN** 115-1 醫學一 is reconciled with its 更正答案 (Q66 答 A 或 D 或 AD 均給分; Q95 一律給分)
- **THEN** Q66 SHALL carry `acceptedAnswers` containing `A` and `D`
- **AND** Q95 SHALL carry `disputed: true`

### Requirement: Content build SHALL satisfy No-Silent-Errors counter discipline

The content build over the extended corpus SHALL print imported / skipped / total question counts. A non-zero skip count SHALL fail the build unless skips are explicitly allowed (`MEDEXAM_ALLOW_SKIPS=1`) AND the skipped items are reported, never silently dropped.

#### Scenario: Build fails loudly on unexplained skips

- **WHEN** the content-neurons-tw build runs over the extended corpus and any question block is skipped
- **THEN** the build SHALL print the imported / skipped / total counts
- **AND** the build SHALL exit non-zero unless `MEDEXAM_ALLOW_SKIPS=1` is set with the skipped items listed

### Requirement: Corpus expansion SHALL be additive with new exam-coordinate ids

Every new question id SHALL be the true exam coordinate `{year}-{session}-{book}-{subject}-Q{qNumber}`. Adding 104/105/115 SHALL NOT change any existing 106–114 id, SHALL NOT require a Dexie `.version()` bump, an R2 bundle `SCHEMA_VERSION` bump, or any sync-adapter change.

#### Scenario: Existing ids unchanged

- **WHEN** the corpus is regenerated with the new sittings
- **THEN** every previously-existing 106–114 question id SHALL still be present and unchanged
- **AND** no Dexie schema version, R2 bundle schema version, or sync adapter SHALL be modified by this change

## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: Explanation provenance SHALL follow one of two documented paths

Each question's explanation SHALL originate from exactly one of two paths, recorded so it is machine-distinguishable:

- **陽明-reconcile path** (sittings with published 陽明 詳解, e.g. 104/105/106–115): `explanation` is the cleaned 陽明 詳解; `sourceCredit` credits 考選部 (試題/答案) + 陽明國考考古題小組 (詳解, CC-BY-NC).
- **AI-generated path** (an individual question 陽明 has not covered — the escape-hatch of "When 陽明 has no 詳解, the 詳解 MAY be AI-generated and tagged"): `explanation` is generated by an LLM; the question SHALL carry `explanationSource: 'ai-generated'`, and `sourceCredit` SHALL state the explanation is AI-generated and NOT 陽明-reviewed.

A question on the 陽明-reconcile path SHALL NOT carry `explanationSource: 'ai-generated'`. As of the 115-1 詳解 publication, 115-1 has been migrated from the AI-generated path to the 陽明-reconcile path **for every question 陽明's published 詳解 booklet covers**; the few 115-1 questions 陽明's curated booklet never wrote a 詳解 for (e.g. the 解剖 questions past the booklet's cut-off) remain on the AI-generated escape-hatch path, still tagged, until 陽明 publishes their 詳解.

#### Scenario: 陽明-covered 115-1 questions are 陽明-reconcile-sourced

- **WHEN** a 115-1 question that 陽明's 詳解 booklet covers is loaded
- **THEN** it SHALL NOT have `explanationSource === 'ai-generated'`
- **AND** its `sourceCredit` SHALL credit 考選部 (試題/答案) + 陽明國考考古題小組 (詳解, CC-BY-NC)

#### Scenario: 115-1 questions 陽明 never covered stay AI-tagged

- **WHEN** a 115-1 question that 陽明's curated 詳解 booklet does not cover is loaded
- **THEN** it MAY retain `explanationSource === 'ai-generated'` and its AI-source `sourceCredit`, and its explanation SHALL NOT be fabricated to a 陽明 source it lacks

#### Scenario: 陽明-path questions are not mislabelled

- **WHEN** a question on the 陽明-reconcile path (any of 104/105/106–114, or a 陽明-covered 115-1 question) with a non-empty explanation is loaded
- **THEN** it SHALL NOT have `explanationSource === 'ai-generated'`

### Requirement: AI-generated explanations SHALL be answer-authoritative, verified, and low-confidence-flagged

Any AI-generated explanation (the escape-hatch path, for a question 陽明 has not covered) SHALL be produced by a generator given the 考選部 authoritative answer, and SHALL produce an explanation that explains why that answer is correct — the generator SHALL NOT be permitted to choose or override the answer. After generation, a second-pass verification SHALL run, and any item whose generated reasoning contradicts the authoritative answer, or which the verification marks uncertain, SHALL be emitted to a low-confidence flag list for human review before the corpus is shipped.

#### Scenario: Generated explanation endorses the authoritative answer

- **WHEN** an explanation is AI-generated for a question whose 考選部 answer is `X`
- **THEN** the generator SHALL receive `X` as the correct answer to explain
- **AND** if the verification pass detects the explanation endorsing an answer other than `X`, that question SHALL be added to the low-confidence flag list

#### Scenario: Low-confidence list gated on human review

- **WHEN** an AI-generation + verification pass completes
- **THEN** a flag list of low-confidence / contradictory items SHALL be produced
- **AND** the corpus SHALL NOT be shipped until those flagged items have been reviewed

### Requirement: 陽明-path explanations SHALL retain the 簡解 Key author tips above the 詳解

陽明-path explanations SHALL retain the author's Key section (簡解 / 答題要訣 / 關鍵字) and present it above the 詳解, and the restoration MUST be a targeted by-id merge on the hand-maintained corpus (never a `reconcile_all.py` regen) that MUST NOT alter `id`, `answer`, stem, or options. The reconcile parser historically emitted only the 詳解 section and dropped this Key.

#### Scenario: A question with a source 簡解 shows it above the 詳解

- **WHEN** a 陽明-path question has a non-empty, meaningful Key section in its source markdown
- **THEN** its reconciled `explanation` SHALL begin with `簡解：`, followed by the NFKC-normalized
  Key text, a divider line, then the original 詳解 unchanged

#### Scenario: Restoration is idempotent and non-duplicating

- **WHEN** a question's explanation already begins with the `簡解：` sentinel, or already contains
  the Key text verbatim
- **THEN** the merge SHALL leave it unchanged with no duplicate 簡解

#### Scenario: Degenerate Keys are not restored

- **WHEN** a source Key is empty, pure punctuation or digits, or a placeholder such as
  「見詳解」or「無」
- **THEN** no 簡解 block SHALL be added for that question

#### Scenario: Out-of-scope questions are left unchanged

- **WHEN** a question belongs to 104/105 (no source Key) or 107-1 or 108-2 (no independent Key), OR still carries `explanationSource === 'ai-generated'` (an AI placeholder — a 陽明 Key SHALL NOT be prepended onto an AI explanation)
- **THEN** its explanation SHALL NOT be modified by 簡解 restoration

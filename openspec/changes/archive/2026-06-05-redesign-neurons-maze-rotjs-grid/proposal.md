## Why

The neurons-tw homepage maze is still structurally built on the four-neurotransmitter-branch model (4 committed graph JSONs `assets/maze/{da,5ht,gaba,glu}-graph.json`, `FAMILY_NT_BRANCH`, 4 per-branch energy pools, 4 branch tint colors). Phase 1 (`drop-neurons-nt-branch-claim-and-synapse-axis`, archived 2026-06-05) only relabelled the player-facing surfaces to neutral text — the indefensible「11 科 = 4 NT 分支」grouping still drives the actual maze topology and economy underneath. A med-student audience scrutinizes neuroscience claims; the 4-branch carve-up of 11 exam subjects has no defensible anatomical basis. This change does the real structural redesign: flatten the 4-branch maze into one unified grid maze whose neuro metaphors are OpenEvidence-grounded and peer-defensible (corridors = axon tracts, the walker = a propagating action potential, intersections = synapses), and make the synapse network — currently a read-only cosmetic overlay — carry real gameplay stake (Hebbian "fire together, wire together").

## What Changes

- **BREAKING (economy reset only)**: the 4 per-branch energy pools (`maze:<branch>:{earned,settles}`) + the 4-branch frontier are reset on the Dexie v16→v17 upgrade. The first-pull `maze:<branch>:starterFamily` keys and all collected variants are **PRESERVED**. Acceptable per owner (few opt-in prod players).
- **Maze spatial model → ONE square, zoomable, complex grid maze** (owner-confirmed). Not 11 separate regions, not a brain-shaped silhouette; cute neural-fiber theme skin. **11 families enter from distinct border cells and converge inward to a shared center (mandatory)**; corridors cross at interior synapse cells (densest at the core). The maze is made as complex as feasible (open-source layout fork / radial center extensions allowed if rot.js generation isn't rich enough).
- **Engine → rot.js** (`rot-js`, BSD-3-Clause, zero-dep, ships TS types) used **headless only**: `ROT.Map.*` (generation), `ROT.Path.Dijkstra` (border→center routing + auto-walk), `ROT.FOV.*` (fog). Own React renderer with neural-fiber tiles — **not** `ROT.Display`. Complexity escape hatch: a forked open-source maze layout may be committed in the same `grid-graph.json` shape. (no-wheels'd: WheelScore 74 🟠. Rejected: Phaser — fights React; PathFinding.js — unlicensed.)
- **Retire the 4 build-time image→graph pipeline + 4 graph JSONs + basemap PNGs**; a single `grid-graph.json` is generated at build time, not by HSV-mask/Zhang-Suen skeletonization of brain images.
- **Auto-navigation + contextual camera (design language)**: walkers auto-advance (idle-walk) inward from the border; movement is a smooth corridor-centerline tween = action-potential propagation (discrete saltatory cell-jump is **parked as a future item**, not the default). **Answering a quiz zooms in to the answered family's walker** (watch it move); **reading shows the whole map + ambient exploration animation**. Pan/zoom supported.
- **Economy → 11 per-family pools** replacing the 4 per-branch pools. New synced meta keys `maze:<family>:earned` + `maze:<family>:settles` × 11 families. A correct answer accrues to the answered subject's own family pool; reading splits across active families. Settle consumes the family's ramped `cost(N)` and triggers one `pullVariant`. **Pacing recalibrated** for 11 fragmented pools (the #1 balance risk vs the prior 4-pool curve + the collection×streak×mastery×acceleration positive-feedback stack).
- **Synapse becomes LOAD-BEARING** (was a read-only overlay): grid intersections where two families' corridors cross are synapses; co-firing the two families strengthens the synapse (per `connectome-collection`, read-only) and grants a capped cross-family bonus (LTP). **No LTD/decay maze mechanic** (owner-cut).
- **Schema**: Dexie `.version(16)` → `.version(17)` (add 11-family `earned/settles` keys; reset 4-branch `earned/settles`; **keep** `starterFamily`) + R2 bundle `SCHEMA_VERSION` 16 → 17 (additive + reader-tolerance). Ships a `db-v16-to-v17` upgrade fixture (required by `lint:dexie-fixtures`).
- **Colors**: 4 `--nt-*` branch tints → a neutral grouping palette carrying no NT taxonomy claim.
- **Out of scope (deferred)**: rewriting the 110 NT-saturated variant personas (change 2 `rewrite-neurons-personas-flat`); the **first-pull rework** (border-onboarding + 二階-style answer-correct-unlock) — a future change. first-pull stays functionally unchanged here (only its `NT_BRANCHES` import source relocates to the content pkg); the new maze reads its legacy `maze:<branch>:starterFamily` keys to light starter nodes.

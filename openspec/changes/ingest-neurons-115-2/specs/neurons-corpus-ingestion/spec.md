### Requirement: Explanation provenance SHALL follow one of two documented paths

Each question's explanation SHALL originate from exactly one of two paths, recorded so it is machine-distinguishable:

- **陽明-reconcile path** (sittings with published 陽明 詳解, e.g. 104/105/106–115-1): `explanation` is the cleaned 陽明 詳解; `sourceCredit` credits 考選部 (試題/答案) + 陽明國考考古題小組 (詳解, CC-BY-NC).
- **AI-generated path** (a question, or a whole sitting, 陽明 has not covered — the escape-hatch of "When 陽明 has no 詳解, the 詳解 MAY be AI-generated and tagged"): `explanation` is generated by an LLM; the question SHALL carry `explanationSource: 'ai-generated'`, and `sourceCredit` SHALL state the explanation is AI-generated and NOT 陽明-reviewed.

A question on the 陽明-reconcile path SHALL NOT carry `explanationSource: 'ai-generated'`. As of the 115-1 詳解 publication, 115-1 has been migrated from the AI-generated path to the 陽明-reconcile path **for every question 陽明's published 詳解 booklet covers**; the few 115-1 questions 陽明's curated booklet never wrote a 詳解 for (e.g. the 解剖 questions past the booklet's cut-off) remain on the AI-generated escape-hatch path, still tagged, until 陽明 publishes their 詳解.

A sitting for which 陽明 has published **no** 詳解 booklet at all SHALL sit wholly on the AI-generated path until such a booklet exists — that is the state 115-1 was in before publication, and the state **115-2** is in now. When a booklet is later published, migration to the 陽明-reconcile path SHALL be additive and SHALL leave AI tags in place on whatever the booklet does not cover.

### Requirement: 更正答案 SHALL map to `disputed` or `acceptedAnswers`

Official 更正答案 SHALL be applied to EVERY included sitting using the existing convention, decoded from that sitting's 答案更正 (`MOD`) file 備註:

- 「一律給分」 / 「除未作答者…其餘均給分」 (any selection correct) SHALL set `disputed: true`.
- 「答Ｘ、Ｙ給分」 / 「答Ｘ或Ｙ…均給分」 (a specific set of letters accepted) SHALL set `acceptedAnswers: [<letters>]` (each individually-credited option letter).
- 「答Ｘ給分」 (single 更正) SHALL set `answer` to the credited option.

This SHALL hold for the official 備註 of every sitting, not only the latest. A question whose official 備註 grants 送分 SHALL NOT be left ungraded-for-送分 (the standard `answer` letter remains the official 標準答案).

The standard answer read from the 標準答案 (`ANS`) grid SHALL be cross-checked before use: a sequential read of the answer cells SHALL agree with a conservative spatial read of the same grid on every cell the spatial reader resolves. The `MOD` grid SHALL be compared cell-by-cell against the `ANS` grid, and the ingest SHALL fail loudly if the 更正 file changes an answer **letter** rather than only marking adjusted cells — a silent letter change would otherwise be indistinguishable from a parse error.

#### Scenario: 115 醫學一 corrections applied

- **WHEN** 115-1 醫學一 is reconciled with its 更正答案 (Q66 答 A 或 D 或 AD 均給分; Q95 一律給分)
- **THEN** Q66 SHALL carry `acceptedAnswers` containing `A` and `D`
- **AND** Q95 SHALL carry `disputed: true`

#### Scenario: 115-2 corrections applied to both books

#### Scenario: Answer-grid reads are cross-checked and letter changes fail loudly

- **WHEN** a sitting's `ANS` and `MOD` answer grids are parsed
- **THEN** the sequential and spatial reads of `ANS` SHALL agree on every spatially-resolved cell
- **AND** if `MOD` differs from `ANS` on any cell by something other than the 更正 marker, the ingest SHALL fail rather than silently adopt the difference

### Requirement: Corpus expansion SHALL be additive with new exam-coordinate ids

Every new question id SHALL be the true exam coordinate `{year}-{session}-{book}-{subject}-Q{qNumber}`. - **AND** every added id SHALL belong to the sitting being ingested

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Ingested question text SHALL be checked against a span-aware parse for detached super/subscripts

`pdftotext -layout` renders a raised super/subscript span as its own output row, which lands at the **end of the preceding line**. The character therefore leaves the word it belongs to and reappears as an orphan token at the tail of the previous stem or option — silently, with no parse error and no failing gate. Any ingest that uses the `-layout` parser SHALL, before the corpus is written, diff every stem and option against a **span-aware** parse that reconstructs each visual line from the PDF's text spans, and SHALL resolve every whitespace-insensitive disagreement in favour of the span-aware text.

The span-aware parse SHALL be used as an **oracle over disagreeing fields only**, never as a blanket replacement: on booklets that carry overlapping duplicate text layers it doubles characters, so adopting it wholesale would trade a visible defect for an invisible one. Adopted text SHALL preserve the corpus's established CJK/ASCII spacing rather than the PDF's own.

#### Scenario: Detached superscripts are detected and repaired before ingest

- **WHEN** a sitting's 試題 PDFs are parsed for ingest
- **THEN** every stem and option SHALL be compared whitespace-insensitively against the span-aware parse
- **AND** each disagreeing field SHALL be written with the span-aware content (e.g. `Cr6+ 的毒性通常較 Cr3+ 強`, not `Cr 的毒性通常較 Cr 強` with `6+ 3+` stranded on the stem)
- **AND** the number of repaired fields SHALL be reported by the ingest, not applied silently

#### Scenario: The span-aware parse is not adopted wholesale

- **WHEN** the span-aware parse and `-layout` agree on a field whitespace-insensitively
- **THEN** the `-layout` text SHALL be kept, so booklets whose duplicate text layers make the span-aware merge double characters are not corrupted by the repair
